# Frozen research protocol

## Object

Boolean functions f:{0,1}^n -> {0,1}, compared under PN equivalence: permutation and independent complementation of input variables. Output complementation is not included.

## Aggregate fingerprint

Phi(f) records:

1. Hamming weight;
2. exact deterministic decision-tree complexity D;
3. sorted pointwise 0-certificate profile;
4. sorted pointwise 1-certificate profile;
5. sorted pointwise sensitivity profile;
6. sorted individual influence profile;
7. algebraic degree;
8. Walsh-Fourier energy aggregated by subset cardinality.

The implementation stores integer Walsh energies to avoid floating-point ambiguity.

## Frozen finite question

Find the smallest n for which there are f and g such that Phi(f)=Phi(g) but f and g are not PN-equivalent.

The repository contains a verified n=5 witness. A publication claim that n=5 is the *smallest* dimension additionally requires a repository-generated exhaustive certificate for n<=4; earlier exploratory calculations are not treated as sufficient archival evidence.

## Structural certificate for the quadratic witness

For degree-at-most-two ANFs, associate a simple graph whose vertices are variables and whose edges are the nonzero quadratic monomials x_i x_j. Input complementation changes only lower-degree terms; variable permutation relabels vertices. Hence non-isomorphic quadratic interaction graphs certify PN inequivalence. The frozen witness uses the stronger elementary certificate that the two graph degree sequences differ.

## The theorem-level target

The finite witness is a starting point, not the final theory. The principal target is an infinite family (f_n,g_n) for which the aggregate fingerprint agrees while PN-invariant interaction structure remains different, preferably with a structural separation that grows with n.

A stronger target is a bounded-order blindness theorem: for every fixed relational order r, construct inequivalent functions agreeing on an explicitly defined family of all allowed <=r-order aggregate signatures but separated at higher relational order.

No such asymptotic theorem is claimed until proved and audited against Boolean matching, quadratic Boolean classification, graph reconstruction/isomorphism, Fourier analysis, and query-complexity literature.

## Evidence policy

A result may be labelled:

- VERIFIED: independently regenerated by committed code and CI;
- COMPUTATIONAL CONJECTURE: observed but not exhaustively certified by the repository;
- THEOREM: accompanied by a mathematical proof;
- NOVELTY CANDIDATE: no exact prior result found after documented search;
- NOVEL: reserved for a completed prior-art audit and a clearly delimited claim.

The paper will be drafted from frozen artifacts, not vice versa.
